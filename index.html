Frequently asked questions

Is the stand self-serve?

Yes. Review the current price board, choose your items, and use the payment option displayed at the stand.

Can we feed the goats?

Hay bags are $2 for fence feeding. Supervised visits inside the goat area are $10 per group for approximately 30–60 minutes and require a completed consent form.

When are classes held?

Dates and availability are posted in the farm calendar. Enrollment details may change by class.

Are children welcome?

Yes, with active adult supervision. Children may not enter animal areas without owner permission and a completed consent form.

Terms, cancellations & farm policies

Farm activities involve uneven ground, weather, plants, hay, tools, and live animals. Visitors must follow posted rules and owner instructions, supervise children, wear appropriate footwear, and avoid entering restricted areas.

Class and visit availability may change because of weather, animal needs, harvest conditions, or safety concerns. Any paid reservation, cancellation, refund, or rescheduling terms should be confirmed directly with the farm before payment.
