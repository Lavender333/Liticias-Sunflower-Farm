Four little personalities

Meet the goats

Each member of the little herd brings a different look to the fence—and plenty of farm-day charm.

Oaky

Oaky is the bright-coated little explorer who loves being the first face visitors see at the fence. His gentle curiosity can turn a simple hello into a favorite farm memory.

Alpine

Alpine is the blue-eyed teddy-bear girl of the herd, with a sweet look that wins hearts right away. She brings a calm, cuddly charm to every farm-day visit.

Willow

Willow is a brown-and-white sweetheart who likes staying close to the herd and watching everything around the farm. When snack time arrives, that quiet curiosity quickly turns into happy excitement.

Cedar

Cedar wears a colorful patterned coat and brings a playful spark wherever the little herd gathers. Always ready for the next bit of farm fun, Cedar makes every visit feel extra cheerful.

Frequently asked questions

Is the stand self-serve?

Yes. Review the current price board, choose your items, and use the payment option displayed at the stand.

Can we visit or feed the goats?

Yes. Mini Visits are $3 per person, with goat feed available for $1 each. Private parties are $25 for 30 minutes or $50 for one hour. Goat Yoga is coming soon. A completed consent form is required before entering an animal area.

When are classes held?

Dates and availability are posted in the farm calendar. Enrollment details may change by class.

Are children welcome?

Yes, with active adult supervision. Children may not enter animal areas without owner permission and a completed consent form.

Privacy notice

Last updated August 27, 2026.

Participation consent forms, guestbook comments, and email-list signups are saved to the farm’s private Google Sheets backend. Completed participation consent forms are also emailed as PDF attachments to the farm’s designated inboxes. Public visitors can read only the current Events and Prices information; private customer records are not exposed.

Form submissions may include names, contact details, emergency contacts, typed signatures, activity choices, and comments. FormSubmit states that archived submissions are retained for 30 days.

Do not enter sensitive medical, financial, or government-identification information. Email addresses are used only for requested farm updates. Subscribers may request removal from the farm’s records.

Terms, cancellations & farm policies

Farm activities involve uneven ground, weather, plants, hay, tools, and live animals. Visitors must follow posted rules and owner instructions, supervise children, wear appropriate footwear, and avoid entering restricted areas.

Class and visit availability may change because of weather, animal needs, harvest conditions, or safety concerns. Any paid reservation, cancellation, refund, or rescheduling terms should be confirmed directly with the farm before payment.

The participation form on this page is a practical record and should be reviewed by the farm’s attorney and insurer before being relied upon as a legal waiver.
